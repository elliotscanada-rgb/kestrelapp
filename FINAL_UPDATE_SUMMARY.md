## 🎬 Carousel Features Explained

### Auto-Play
- Rotates automatically every 5 seconds
- Pauses when you hover
- Resumes when you move mouse away

### Navigation Arrows
- Click left arrow to go to previous slide
- Click right arrow to go to next slide
- Arrows scale up on hover
- Smooth transitions between images

### Dot Indicators
- Shows current slide position
- Click any dot to jump to that slide
- Active dot highlighted in gold
- All dots clickable

### Captions
- Text description for each image
- Appears at bottom of slide
- Gradient overlay for readability
- Professional typography
